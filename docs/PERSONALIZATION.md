# Personal learning plan

Open **Progress → Your learning plan**. Confirm up to three goals: introductions, making plans, asking for help. Save/reset goals without AI. Dismissed suggestions stay hidden; resetting clears goals/dismissals, while retained analysis evidence remains inspectable. No automatic enrollment into FSRS.

**Analyze saved contexts** explicitly sends up to 30 owned saved English excerpts to the configured Gemini text provider. URLs and whole pages are excluded. Ten distinct new contexts of at least 20 characters are required; normalization prevents repeated sentences from inflating eligibility. Latest 500 successful English lookups are sampled. The AI may suggest fewer than three scenarios or none; confidence is uncalibrated, not an established fact. Every evidence ID must belong to that batch. Learners inspect excerpts and accept/dismiss goals. Unchanged context snapshots reuse the saved result. Normalized context hashes define freshness; excerpts collected during generation remain eligible for the next request. Two simultaneous analyses and six new calls/minute per process are allowed; same-user requests coalesce. Errors leave the profile untouched. Local text mode makes no cloud scenario calls; manual goals remain available.

Suggestions never overwrite confirmed goals. Writes lock and recheck the owned account/consent generation after model work; deletion or changed collection settings reject late replies. The user profile is included in account export and cascaded account deletion.

Priority v1 is deterministic: confirmed-goal keyword clues (30%), personal encounters (25%), available subtitle frequency (20%), reliable independent item-linked practice gap (15%), active due review (10%). Missing signals remain null and add no priority contribution on a fixed evidence scale. They are not an ability judgment. Removing their weights would reward missing frequency; that normalization was removed after the learner audit. Exact formulas are in `personalization/priority.ts`. Goal matching uses a small inspectable English keyword list; phrases/words outside the frequency snapshot remain **unknown**, not rare or unlearned. Practice gap needs two different independent scored tasks from up to 500 vocabulary-practice attempts in the last 30 days; v2 speech also requires clear audio. Self-checks, prompted repeats and unreliable recordings do not count. The first five priorities are shown by default; learners can expand to twenty. Practice links preserve the requested listening/speaking mode, prefer a matched confirmed scenario and return to Progress. Numeric evidence points live in the explanation rather than appearing as a learner grade.

The adapted [FrequencyWords/OpenSubtitles snapshot notice](../apps/api/src/personalization/NOTICE.md) pins source, license, transformations and offline reproduction. Subtitle-domain evidence is shown with its attribution on the page. Scores express practice priority, not mastery or CEFR level. There is no RAG or full browsing-history ingestion.

The API reports `analysis.status`, `newContexts` and `minimum`; the button uses fresh-context eligibility, not total history size. AI confidence percentages are not presented as calibrated probabilities. Unsaved goals survive refresh; conflicting goal changes require reloading the saved goals. Optional `expectedGoals` protects against stale writes from another tab.
